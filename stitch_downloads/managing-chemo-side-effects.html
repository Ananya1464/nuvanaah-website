Skin Health

Managing Chemotherapy Side Effects on Your Skin

By Dr. Meera Iyer November 12, 2024

The journey of healing is profound, demanding grace and profound self-compassion. As your body undergoes the necessary rigors of chemotherapy, the canvas of your skin often reflects this internal battle. It is a time when the gentle touch of a soothing cream becomes more than just a routine; it transforms into a vital ritual of care and reverence for the vessel carrying you through this process.

Embracing the Delicate Shift

It is entirely normal to notice a shift in your skin's texture and temperament. Increased sensitivity, dryness, and a newfound fragility are common companions during this phase. The approach to skincare must pivot from correction to deep, nurturing protection. Think of your skin as a delicate silk fabric—it requires gentle handling and the softest of elements to maintain its integrity.

check_circle The Gentle Practices

  • water_drop Lukewarm Waters: Opt for tepid baths to prevent stripping natural oils.
  • spa Fragrance-Free Nourishment: Apply unscented, rich moisturizers immediately after bathing.
  • checkroom Soft Embraces: Wear breathable, natural fabrics like organic bamboo or soft cotton.
  • local_drink Internal Hydration: Drink ample water throughout the day to support cellular healing.

cancel Practices to Pause

  • hot_tub Hot Showers: High temperatures can severely exacerbate dryness and irritation.
  • back_hand Harsh Exfoliants: Skip scrubs, loofahs, and any abrasive skincare tools.
  • liquor Alcohol-Based Products: Avoid toners and astringents that contain drying alcohols.
  • wb_sunny Direct Sun Exposure: Protect sensitive skin rigorously with broad-spectrum SPF and physical barriers.
