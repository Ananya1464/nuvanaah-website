How to Choose the Right Breast Prosthesis

A guide to finding comfort, balance, and confidence on your healing journey.

The journey to finding the right breast prosthesis is deeply personal. It's not just about restoring physical symmetry; it's about reclaiming your comfort, your posture, and your sense of self. With so many options available, understanding the nuances of weight, material, and fit is essential.

Understanding Your Needs

Every woman's body and recovery timeline is unique. In the immediate weeks following surgery, a lightweight, soft form is often recommended to protect sensitive healing tissue. As you progress, transitioning to a weighted silicone form can provide the necessary balance to prevent shoulder and back discomfort.

Key Considerations for Your Perfect Fit

  • check
    1. Weight & Balance Ensuring natural balance and posture. A properly weighted prosthesis mimics the natural breast, preventing muscular strain on the opposite side of the body.
  • check
    2. Material Matters The benefits of high-grade, temperature-regulating silicone. Look for materials that breathe and adapt to your body heat for all-day comfort.
  • check
    3. Shape & Fit How to match your unique shape and movement. Whether symmetrical, asymmetrical, teardrop, or oval, the right shape ensures a seamless look under clothing.

The Importance of Professional Fitting

While guidelines are helpful, nothing replaces the expertise of a certified fitter. They can assess your surgical site, lifestyle needs, and body mechanics to recommend options you might not have considered. We recommend scheduling a fitting 6-8 weeks post-surgery, once swelling has significantly reduced.
